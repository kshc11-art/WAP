v0.4.1 LOSSLESS-FIRST + SPACING NORMALIZATION
================================================

1. Source physical HWP cell content is selected with MoveToField + SelectAll.
2. Source content is pasted into a temporary HWP document.
3. Only empty paragraphs on the temporary document's TOP-LEVEL List are removed.
4. Nested table/control Lists are not edited.
5. Control-looking paragraphs (position span >= 8) are not deleted.
6. The repeated [heading] paragraph is removed from the temporary copy.
7. Remaining HWP payload is copied natively and pasted into the same destination field.
8. Destination content is never cleared/rebuilt.
9. Duplicate topics are never automatically deleted.

10. Date cleanup first handles Y.M.D (leading-zero month/day), then standalone M.D.
